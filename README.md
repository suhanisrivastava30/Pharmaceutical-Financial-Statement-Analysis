# Pharmaceutical Financial Statement Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses the financial performance of Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited over five financial years, from FY 2021–22 to FY 2025–26. Using Microsoft Excel, the project examines financial statements, key financial ratios, potential red flags, management commentary, audit findings, notes to accounts, and Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) performance.

## Business Problem / Objective
The objective is to assess the company's financial performance, profitability, financial position, and potential areas of risk. The analysis aims to identify changes in financial trends, evaluate the quality of reported earnings, and understand the company's financial and non-financial performance.

## Project Context
Sun Pharma operates in the pharmaceutical industry, where financial performance is influenced by research and development, regulatory requirements, product launches, acquisitions, and market conditions. A five-year analysis helps examine changes in the company's financial position and identify areas that require further investigation.

## My Role / Personal Contribution

## Approach
- Analysed consolidated profit and loss statements and balance sheets for five financial years.
- Examined financial ratios and year-on-year changes in key performance indicators.
- Evaluated potential financial red flags using defined checks and conditions.
- Reviewed management discussion and analysis (MD&A) to understand the reasons behind significant financial movements.
- Examined notes to accounts, statutory audit findings, and ESG indicators.
- Developed insights from the combined financial and non-financial analysis.

## Tools / Methods
- **Microsoft Excel:** Financial data organisation, formulas, calculations, and analysis.
- **Financial Ratio Analysis:** Assessment of growth, profitability, returns, and other financial indicators.
- **Trend Analysis:** Comparison of financial performance across five years.
- **Red Flag Analysis:** Identification of unusual financial movements and potential areas of concern.
- **Qualitative Analysis:** Review of management commentary, accounting disclosures, and audit reports.
- **ESG Analysis:** Examination of environmental, social, and governance trends.

## Key Insight / Recommendation
The workbook indicates that Sun Pharma's revenue from operations increased from approximately ₹386.5 billion in FY 2021–22 to ₹584.6 billion in FY 2025–26. However, revenue growth alone does not provide a complete picture of financial performance.

The analysis also highlights trade receivables growing faster than revenue in FY 2025–26, making working capital management and collection efficiency important areas to monitor. The five-year ESG analysis also indicates a decline in Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emission intensity.

**Recommendation:** Continue monitoring profitability, cash conversion, receivable collection periods, and financial red flags alongside revenue growth. Interpret automated red-flag results in the context of financial statements, disclosures, and management explanations.
